Vanguard Learn

Mobile app for tracking curriculum progress and skills, with an AI tutor. Designed in Figma.

Project Mobile app for curriculum and skill tracking
Timeline September 2026
Platform Mobile
Tools Figma
Figma Prototype

Context

Vanguard Learn is an EdTech concept that combines academic advising, curriculum tracking and skill tracking.

Final Design

Competency Radar

Shows five skill areas in one view, so a student can see their strongest and weakest areas at a glance.

Streaks

Tracks daily study habits over 14 days.

AI Tutor

Asks guiding questions instead of giving answers.

Figures such as 82% readiness are sample data in the mockup.
